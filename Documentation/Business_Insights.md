## AdventureWorks Excel Analytics

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The analysis of AdventureWorks sales data identified several key business opportunities and performance areas. Overall sales performance was strong in terms of revenue generation, with total sales of approximately **$77.55 million** being the most significant driver of business performance. However, the analysis also identified weak profitability in several high-sales products and categories, particularly the **Bikes category**, which generated approximately **$64.07 million in sales but a negative profit of $435.68K**. This represents an important area requiring immediate attention.

From a product and category perspective, the **Mountain-200 product family** was the strongest contributor to sales and absolute profit. **Mountain-200 Black, 38** was the top-selling product, generating approximately **$2.94 million in sales** and **$272.77K in profit**. However, several high-sales products generated negative profit margins, presenting potential opportunities for pricing, cost, and product-mix improvements.

Geographically, the **Southwest region** was the leading market, generating approximately **$18.00 million** and contributing approximately **23% of total sales**. Other regions with lower sales but positive profitability may offer additional growth opportunities.

The reseller analysis showed that the business has a relatively diversified reseller network. The **top 10 resellers contributed approximately 9.98% of total sales**, indicating low dependency on a small number of reseller partners. **Brakes and Gears** was the strongest individual reseller by both sales and profit.

Salesperson analysis identified **Linda Mitchell** as the leading salesperson by sales, generating approximately **$10.16 million**, while **Jae Pak** generated the highest profit at approximately **$264.95K**. This demonstrates that revenue leadership and profitability leadership are not always aligned.

Target performance analysis identified a significant gap between actual sales and assigned targets. The available target analysis reported approximately **$77.55 million in actual sales against $676.21 million in total targets**, resulting in overall achievement of approximately **11.47%**. This requires further investigation into target-setting methodology, data completeness, and sales execution.

Based on these findings, the business should prioritize:

1. Improving profitability in high-sales, low-profit products and categories.
2. Investigating the significant gap between actual sales and assigned targets.
3. Expanding profitable growth opportunities across high-margin products, regions, and reseller partners.

These actions can help improve revenue quality, profitability, target achievement, and long-term business performance.

# 2. Overall Business Performance

The business generated approximately **$77.55 million in total sales** based on the analyzed dataset.

The overall analysis shows that the company has a strong revenue base but faces important profitability challenges. High sales volume does not always translate into strong profitability.

Several products, categories, and reseller relationships generate significant revenue while producing weak or negative profit. This indicates that the business should focus not only on increasing sales but also on improving the quality and profitability of those sales.

### Key Performance Observations

- Total Sales: **$77.55M**
- Strong revenue generation across the product portfolio.
- Significant profitability differences across products and categories.
- Bikes generated the highest sales but recorded negative overall profit in the analyzed results.
- Top 10 products contributed **25.35% of total sales**.
- Top 10 resellers contributed only **9.98% of total sales**.
- Salesperson revenue and profitability leadership were not fully aligned.
- Target achievement analysis showed a significant gap between actual sales and assigned targets.

### Business Implication

The business should move from a purely revenue-focused approach toward a balanced performance framework based on:

**Sales + Profit + Profit Margin + Target Achievement**

This provides a more complete view of business performance.

# 3. Sales Trend Insights

- The lowest sales month was **June**, with approximately **$3.13 million** in sales.
- The strongest profit month was **November**, generating approximately **$255.25K** in profit.
- The weakest profit month was **June**, with approximately **-$366.97K** in profit.

### Insight

Sales performance increased significantly across the analyzed years, but monthly performance fluctuated.

The difference between the strongest and weakest months suggests that the business may experience seasonal demand patterns or variations in product and market performance.

### Business Implication

Management should investigate recurring monthly patterns and use them to improve:

- Inventory planning
- Promotional timing
- Sales resource allocation
- Product availability
- Reseller support

- Target Variance: **-$598.66M**
- Overall Achievement: approximately **11.47%**

### Top Target Performer

**Jae Pak**

Target Achievement: **59.67%**

Among the listed salespeople, Jae Pak recorded the highest target achievement rate.

### Largest Negative Variance

**Brian Welcker**

Variance: **-$221.70M**

No actual sales were recorded for Brian Welcker in the available target performance data, while a large target was assigned.

### Important Data Quality Observation

The target analysis contains unusual target values and incomplete actual sales records. Therefore, the results should be interpreted carefully.

The extremely low overall achievement rate may reflect:

- Unrealistically high targets
- Missing actual sales records
- Differences in target and sales date ranges
- Incomplete salesperson mappings
- Data quality issues

### Recommendation

Before using target achievement for formal performance evaluation, management should validate:

- Target values
- Salesperson mappings
- Date ranges
- Missing sales records
- Target assignment logic

- Sales support

Protect strategic partners such as Brakes and Gears while improving the profitability of high-sales/low-profit resellers.

- Historical performance
